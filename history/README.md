# Historical atlas — 1 January 2018 to 17 November 2026

A **geographic zoom** through documented pre-divergence history of The New World. Begin with the widest context, then follow the chain to the region under development. At each depth, the same real-world event is explained only to the extent that it changes the institutions, people, economy, resources and infrastructure at that scale.

**Cutoff:** 17 November 2026. **Alternate history begins:** convincing public AGI announcement in December 2026. **Future scenarios:** widespread automation and labor displacement in 2027–2029; a distinct Great Plague around 2030; Cascade around 2034–2035, provisional. No claims after the cutoff are recorded as actual history.

## Research principles

1. **Scale:** global = representative worldwide forces; continental = cross-border systems; national = institutions; West = geographic resource networks; state = laws, disasters and economy; local = real communities, roads, water, employers, clinics and hazard maps.
2. **Independent sources:** primary agencies and dated accounts are preferred. Numbers need a baseline year and source. Factual events and later interpretations should be distinguishable.
3. **Causation isn't destiny:** a 2018 tariff, 2020 pandemic or 2025 fire can affect the baseline without automatically causing a fictional 2030 outbreak or the solar Cascade.
4. **No invented local history:** historical events at unresolved Wyoming ranch/reservation coordinates are omitted rather than assigned to a guess.
5. **Time:** documented Gregorian dates are authoritative. The Studio currently only supports approximate Cascade-relative year fields for some timeline events; do not confuse those approximations with the exact calendar date.
6. **No duplicated content:** every article is a source document with its own stable ID, and structured \`narrows_to\` relationships form the hierarchy. They are not copied into ten unrelated entries. A reader can follow published relationships after editorial approval; importing is not publishing.

## Source provenance

Each article includes its own citations to WHO, IMF, World Bank, UN agencies, US Census, NOAA, EIA, CAL FIRE, state agencies, local water districts and county hazard plans as appropriate. Sources reporting 2025/2026 activity are restricted to information available by **17 November 2026**. Prior historical summaries may be corrected as new source checks emerge, but alternate-history canon cannot be silently treated as historical fact.

## Future research priorities

The Wyoming local study is blocked on identifying Diamond Ranch and the reservation. All three starting regions still need street- and settlement-level hospital inventories, demographic age groups, exact water infrastructure and well depths, farming capacity, businesses, transport dependencies and reviewed economic data. These should be added to *separate place entries* after sources are checked, not invented in a master overview.

**Software docs:** The Studio architecture and UX design files remain outside this historical atlas and are never imported as in-world history.

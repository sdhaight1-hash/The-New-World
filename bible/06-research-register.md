# Research register and unresolved decisions

**Priority 1: global baseline**
- Establish realistic epidemiological **qualitative** constraints and wave chronology, mortality-versus-migration accounting, metropolitan connectivity and urban/rural exceptions.
- Confirm 2030 tournament chronology and whether it is the fictional epidemic's amplifier rather than its origin.
- Set provisional year-by-year global timeline and when the plague is mostly over relative to the Cascade.

**Priority 2: physical realism**
- Decide explicitly between largely *operational* vehicle loss and the additional fictional widespread direct electronic failures previously discussed. Solar storms alone do not support mass direct car failures.
- Define plausible recurring geomagnetic event magnitude, protected local-grid survival and infrastructure restoration bottlenecks.
- Model ammunition/fuel longevity, small-scale production potential, salvage limits, steam and animal-power adoption.

**Priority 3: Southern California**
- Analyze mobility through Cajon Pass and alternate mountain roads; distinguish LA–IE connectedness from Victor Valley and Big Bear visitor patterns.
- Model surface water, aquifers and pump feasibility; distinguish Big Bear Lake from drinking-water sources.
- Reconstruct populations at pre-plague, pre-Cascade, Year 0, Year 5 and Year 50.
- Identify plausible agricultural hinterlands and markets supporting Big Bear and desert holdouts.

**Priority 4: Clatsop**
- Study coastal agricultural carrying capacity, fisheries, port and river trade, forest products and pandemic travel pathways.
- Develop settlement-level epidemic histories without assuming uniform survival.

**Priority 5: Wyoming**
- Locate the specific Diamond Ranch and reservation before writing definitive geographic or political history.
- Study rights, rivers, seasonal constraints, ranch economies, land and community institutions.

**Priority 6: historical coherence**
- Build linked entries for actual institutions, evolving settlement populations, trade routes, property systems, leaders and families only after baseline modeling.
- Track provenance for real-world facts, worldbuilding assumptions and later revisions. Leave political structures open rather than filling the map with arbitrary kingdoms.

## Entry status vocabulary

- **Established:** creator-approved directional constraint, not necessarily fully quantified.
- **Working:** explanatory hypothesis or candidate history awaiting evidence or approval.
- **Open:** no definitive outcome.
- **Superseded:** explicitly replaced idea, preserved to prevent reintroduction.

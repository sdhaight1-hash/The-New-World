# The Cascade and the Long Storms

**Status:** catastrophe and decades-long recurrence established in the setting; physical severity and mechanisms need careful qualification.

## Event

After the Great Plague has largely waned and societies have begun recovery, successive exceptionally severe solar events strike Earth. Geomagnetic activity damages vulnerable high-voltage networks and interferes with satellites, navigation and radio systems, producing cascading loss of pumping, fuel supply, refrigeration, medicine distribution, finance, transport and industrial coordination. Further extreme storms return intermittently for decades, repeatedly threatening attempts to restore vulnerable long-distance electrical infrastructure.

**Scientific boundary:** solar flares and coronal mass ejections are not synonymous. Geomagnetic storms from Earth-directed CMEs chiefly couple to large conductive systems; direct destruction of most cars or isolated electronics worldwide is **not** a normal consequence. If the world needs widespread immediate car failures, specify a separate extraordinary fictional electromagnetic mechanism and track it honestly; otherwise most cars disappear operationally through scarcity, fuel delivery, batteries, repairs and supply-chain collapse. This is an **open canon decision**.

## What is not changed

- Chemistry, combustion, explosives, human biology, mechanics and thermodynamics function normally.
- Firearms continue functioning; ammunition can survive long storage and be reloaded or manufactured under suitable conditions.
- Gasoline/diesel and internal-combustion engines remain workable in principle, though fuel degrades or runs out and supply networks fail.
- Steam, sail, wind, water, muscle power and mechanical transmission remain viable.
- Some local electricity may survive or be rebuilt. Recurrence increases risks; it does not prohibit all power.

## Long-term implications

Persistent extreme space weather discourages some grid architectures and can encourage disconnected, shielded and manually operated systems. Regions with fuel, specialized workers, repairable generators and materials can rebuild unevenly. Do **not** assume an instantaneous, permanently uniform medieval technological level.

## Research watchpoints

- Space-weather recurrence: ~11-year solar activity cycle is real; repeated planet-wide civilization-threatening storms for decades are an extraordinary fictional concentration, not a forecast.
- Distribution-grid versus transmission-grid vulnerability and varying regional exposure.
- Water systems, supply-chain collapse, machinery salvage, transformer replacement and industrial dependencies.
- Consequences for agriculture, shipping, medicine, rail and communications.

# Genealogy, kinship and dynastic succession

**Status:** architecture adopted; historical family entries and political houses not yet created.

In Year 50 America, parentage and inherited relationships can influence land, marriage alliances, military obligations, social standing and political legitimacy. But **family =/= house =/= office =/= estate**. These must remain independent records, historically dated and linked with explicit claims.

## Domains

### Individuals and families

Stable person identifiers; birth/death and place; names at different times; biological, adoptive, step, legal and disputed parentage; marriages, dissolved unions, guardianship, kinship, household affiliation and migration. People may belong to more than one household or house over a lifetime.

### Houses and lineages

A house is a political/social institution with a name, founding history, emblem, members, branches, territorial associations and competing legitimacy claims. A house is **not** the same thing as all descendants of one surname. Women, adopted children, collateral lines and nonbiological successors may inherit depending on local customs.

### Estates and succession

Ownership/tenure of a farm or estate, membership in a house and holding a public office are distinct events. A ruler might inherit a title while land passes elsewhere. Document the inheritance rule *for that jurisdiction and period*: appointment, election, primogeniture, partition, seniority, designation, customary agreement, adjudication or conquest.

### Historical evidentiary records

Track each assertion's **valid-time interval** (when it happened) and **research/canon status** (established, working, disputed, rejected). Link to world-bible entries, chronicles, wills, censuses, oral histories and treaties. Different historical accounts may conflict. Do not erase contradictions; resolve or preserve them explicitly.

## Historical consistency checks

- No person precedes themself through a parentage cycle.
- Warn, but do not automatically forbid, unusually small age gaps, implausible lifespan or dates conflicting with asserted birth/death.
- Detect contradictions in exclusive offices or property possession, but allow competing disputed claims.
- Do not assume an individual's legal status from biological descent.
- Recompute family and office status at a selected historical date, not only Year 50.
- Never invent parents, children, marriages or hereditary claims merely to complete a tree.

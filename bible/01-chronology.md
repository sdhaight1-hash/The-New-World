# Master chronology — working history

This chronology has a **documented historical segment** from November 30, 2022, through November 17, 2026, followed by an **alternate-history divergence in December 2026**. Real dates in the documented portion are established historical events. December 2026 onward follows The New World's created history, with exact future details still open. The proposed 2030 Plague and 2034–2035 Cascade dates remain provisional. The 2030 men's World Cup is a scheduled real-world event, not proof that it causes or amplifies the fictional epidemic.

The world's opening historical period is [**The Brief Age of AI (2022–2030)**](20-brief-age-of-ai.md). It precedes the two independent physical catastrophes, explaining how displacement of labor, concentration of ownership and fragile institutions shape the societies that encounter them.

## Historical causality to preserve

**Public conversational AI → agentic automation → December 2026 AGI announcement → mass displacement of human labor and widening institutional strain → international travel and epidemic seeding → asymmetric mortality and pre-Cascade migrations → partial recovery with weakened staffing/institutions → later independent solar shocks → fragmented industry/logistics → unequal local adaptation → diverse successor societies.**

The large-scale AI labor crisis belongs specifically to the **fictional 2027–2029 period**; it is not asserted as an established fact of the real world as of November 17, 2026. Software and robotics have different infrastructure requirements; some AI systems can survive on local hardware after later network failures.

Not every survivor moved, and a region losing most of its *population* need not have the same percentage of *deaths*: distinguish direct plague mortality, later secondary deaths, births and out-migration.

## Major regional anchors to investigate

- **Southern California:** epidemic connectivity of LAX/LA basin/Orange County/western Inland Empire; limited mountain transport crossings; Cajon quarantine; Big Bear resort and water constraints; Victor Valley wells, river corridor, holdouts and migration.
- **Clatsop County:** ports and tourism exposure; coastal fisheries, wetland soils, forest, river valleys, links to Columbia trade.
- **Wyoming:** identify actual ranch/reservation geography first; ranching, water rights, local government and tribal sovereignty.

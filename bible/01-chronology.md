## Historical causality to preserve

**International travel and epidemic seeding → asymmetric mortality and pre-Cascade migrations → partial recovery with weakened staffing/institutions → later solar shocks → fragmented industry/logistics → unequal local adaptation → diverse successor societies.**

Not every survivor moved, and a region losing most of its *population* need not have the same percentage of *deaths*: distinguish direct plague mortality, later secondary deaths, births and out-migration.

## Major regional anchors to investigate

- **Southern California:** epidemic connectivity of LAX/LA basin/Orange County/western Inland Empire; limited mountain transport crossings; Cajon quarantine; Big Bear resort and water constraints; Victor Valley wells, river corridor, holdouts and migration.
- **Clatsop County:** ports and tourism exposure; coastal fisheries, wetland soils, forest, river valleys, links to Columbia trade.
- **Wyoming:** identify actual ranch/reservation geography first; ranching, water rights, local government and tribal sovereignty.

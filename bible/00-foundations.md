# Foundations and canon ledger

## Established direction

- **Purpose:** global conworlding and an evolving historical atlas/encyclopedia, *not* presently a novel or fixed storyline.
- **Setting:** real-world Earth in an alternate near-future history, chiefly examined around **fifty years after the Cascade**.
- **Dual catastrophe:** a globally devastating Great Plague begins years before the solar disaster; a later sequence of intense solar disturbances devastates infrastructure, with exceptionally destructive activity recurring over subsequent decades. The two are **unrelated in origin**.
- **Plague geography:** early travel through large international airports and interlinked metros contributes to extremely high losses in many major population centers. Rural and more easily isolated communities often fare better because warnings, visible illness and feasible travel restrictions allow local containment. **Not every city falls and not every rural community survives.**
- **Post-collapse physics:** ordinary chemistry and mechanics remain intact. Guns, fuel engines, electronics and steam remain physically possible; fuel supplies, ammunition, manufacturing, energy networks, replacement parts, and personnel become scarce and uneven.
- **Social trajectory:** localized power and landholding arrangements can become hereditary and feudal-like, but republics, tribal governments, town councils, federations and industrial enclaves may also persist.
- **Initial areas to detail:** (1) Big Bear–Victor Valley and connected Southern California, (2) Clatsop County including Astoria, Seaside and Cannon Beach, (3) Wyoming country between the user-described Diamond Ranch and a reservation. These are **not** exclusive areas.

## The opening era — established setting direction

- **Historical beginning:** January 1, 2018. The timeline begins with the pre-pandemic world, US–China trade tensions and major 2018 political, technological and disaster developments, followed by late-2019 COVID-19 emergence and the subsequent pandemic, economic and political changes, regional wars, global trade, climate, energy, technology and other influential developments. ChatGPT’s public release on November 30, 2022 is a major technological milestone, not the beginning of the timeline.
- **Documented cutoff:** real history through November 17, 2026, including increasing AI capabilities and adoption, but without asserting real economy-wide labor displacement.
- **First major alternate-history divergence:** a convincing worldwide AGI announcement in **December 2026**. Exact day, announcing party and implementation remain open.
- **Brief AI and automation age:** 2027–2029 sees intense, globally uneven deployment and large-scale loss or transformation of human jobs. Economic productivity and private capital concentrations can rise alongside weakened wages, careers and local fiscal capacity.
- **Causal role:** the later Great Plague begins amid existing employment upheaval and social strain; AI is not the pathogen's cause, and the separate Cascade physically disrupts infrastructure sustaining many automated systems. Some offline/local AI technology can survive.

See [the 2018–2026 geographic historical atlas](../history/README.md), [the 2019–2026 background chapter](21-real-world-2019-2026.md) and [The Brief Age of AI](20-brief-age-of-ai.md).

## Provisional (not locked)

- **2030** men's World Cup as early international amplification event for the plague; outbreak timeline and biological traits unmodeled.
- Plague crisis roughly **2030–2032**, significant decline/recovery around **2032–2033**, first Cascade around **2034–2035**; Year 50 roughly **2084–2085**.
- LA/parts of Inland Empire experience exceptional mortality and subsequent migration, while Big Bear and Victor Valley preserve comparatively more population through exposure timing, travel restrictions, containment and chance.
- Concentrated controls around Cajon Pass and other mountain access routes help create a regional epidemic divide.
- Big Bear and Victor Valley together become an important southern California successor hub; the desert retains smaller holdout communities; some migrants continue north into agricultural California.
- Massive mortality and decline of metropolitan systems enable a large political and demographic shift toward smaller communities.

## Explicitly rejected or superseded

- A supernatural or chemical “Suppression Field” disabling gunpowder, engines or electronics.
- A required fantasy awakening or magical alteration of physics.
- All vehicles and guns automatically cease working forever.
- Victor Valley entirely abandoned or removed from the primary Southern California focus.
- Worldbuilding limited to three enclosed regions.
- A mandatory single novel plot or uniform feudal states everywhere.

## Core realism boundary

Real geomagnetic storms primarily threaten large conductive systems and satellite/radio operations. Their destruction of most disconnected car electronics is **not a standard scientific consequence**. The setting must either (a) make large-scale vehicle loss arise predominantly through loss of fuel, service, replacement parts and functioning infrastructure, or (b) explicitly designate a separate fictional electromagnetic effect. **Not yet resolved; do not imply it is ordinary solar physics.**

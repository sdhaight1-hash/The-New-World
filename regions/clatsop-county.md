# Northern Oregon — Clatsop County focus

**Status:** initial geographic focus, not a closed region. Primary localities: Astoria, Warrenton, Gearhart, Seaside, Cannon Beach, inland valleys and Columbia/Pacific connections.

## Geographic and economic baseline to document

Columbia River estuary and maritime access; Pacific coastline; coastal rainfall and freshwater; timber/forests; fisheries; lowland soils, flood risk and limited agricultural land; US 101 and routes through the Coast Range. Port facilities and boatbuilding/repair may matter immensely, but fishing still needs boats, maintenance, labor, storage and preserved catch.

## Plague history: not decided

Astoria has port and regional road links; Seaside and Cannon Beach have tourism exposure; inland settlements differ in access and mobility. Investigate whether outbreaks are concentrated in accessible/coastal towns, which roads close, and which rural districts preserve the population needed for Year 50 institutions. Do not presume all of Clatsop avoids infection.

## Cascade and Year 50: working questions

Does the region preserve boatbuilding and sailing? Which estuary crossings and coastal routes remain safe? How are winter food supplies, salt, preservation, grazing and timber managed? What institutions take over port, land and market responsibilities? Could commercial towns, agricultural estates and council-governed settlements coexist? **No borders, ruling families or invented casualty numbers have yet been assigned.**

## External connections

Lower Columbia and southwest Washington, Willamette Valley, coastal trade and inland timber/agricultural routes. All can be developed when relevant.
